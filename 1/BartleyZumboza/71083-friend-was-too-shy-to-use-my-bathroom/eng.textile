Friend was too shy to use my bathroom

Author: BartleyZumboza

*000*2021-10-07 22:58

So, I recently posted my introduction. I explained that I’m a writer and I’ll eventually be sharing some stories. Some will be fictional and some will be real experiences. Here’s one that happened to me recently that I thought I’d share 🙂

I have a friend who we’ll call Ellie. We’ve been friends since high school, and despite how different our personalities are, we never abandon each other and we’re always there for each other when we need a friend. We’ve tried to date each other a few times in the past, but the chemistry has just never been there and I’ve never really thought of her in a sexual way. She has never had a boyfriend (until very recently when she met someone who’s more on her level than I am, and their relationship is fucking adorable), whereas I’ve been in quite a few relationships over the course of our friendship.

A few days ago, Ellie came over to watch some anime. We started the day hanging out at a local breakfast place, around 10 AM, where we both had a few cups of coffee. After paying, we drove to my apartment, about 10 minutes down the road. This was her first time visiting me at my apartment. I usually go to her place when we hang out, but we decided to switch things up this time.

Now, I live in a studio apartment with a narrow space between the bed and the bathroom. This is very convenient if you need to go to the bathroom during the night. Just get up, walk three steps, and the toilet is right there. The bathroom has a sliding door, but it’s paper thin and anyone in the room can hear everything that’s happening in there. The room is also fairly compact, so the couch, the TV and the bed are all fairly close together. Typically, when I watch Netflix, I place my laptop on the bed, where the HDMI can easily reach, and sit on the couch.

Ellie and I started out sitting on the couch. We’re in the middle of binging Soul Eater together and we’re close to the end. But we have quite a few episodes to go, so we waste no time. While we’re on the couch, I offer her a big bottle of water, and drink one myself. During this time, I go to the bathroom twice. She hasn’t gone yet.

At a certain point, I decided to move over to my bed to recline on my stomach and more easily reach the laptop to skip the openings. At this point I notice that my friend is starting to show some subtle signs of needing to pee. She’s shifting her legs around, fidgeting, and starting to move closer to the edge of her seat. There are several times when she looks like she’s about to get up, but stops short. As someone who's been into this kind of thing all my life, I'm pretty good at picking up on when someone needs to pee, so as much as she tried to hide it, it's obvious to someone like me.

I wondered why she didn't just get up and go, but it started to occur to me: she’s too shy to pee when I’m lying on the bed right next to the bathroom. She’s always been pretty shy, but we’ve known each other for a decade so I feel like it shouldn’t matter. Then again, this is her first time visiting my apartment, so I understand that she might be uncomfortable in the unfamiliar environment. On one hand, I felt bad that my friend was shy about using the bathroom around me, but on the other, this was the perfect opportunity to be, to quote Freaky Fred, “naughty.”

So I decided to have a little fun. And in this case, “fun” just means continuing on hanging out normally and pretending I didn’t know my friend really needed to go to the bathroom. I continued lying on the bed and making comments about the show. We continued chilling and watching as normal. During this time, I peed again, twice. I honestly could have held it, but my devious mind wanted to torment her bladder a little bit, so I pissed full-force, directly into the bowl, making it as loud as I possibly could. At this point, we’ve had the same amount to drink. A LOT to drink. And I’ve now peed FOUR times and she hasn’t peed once. I imagined how she must have felt hearing me empty my bladder several times while hers was still very full.

Keep in mind, I wasn’t actively preventing her from using the bathroom. She could get up and go anytime she wanted, but it was just her own shyness keeping herself from the relief she wanted. She hadn’t expressed any need to use the bathroom and she wasn’t making any moves to go, so for all she knew, I was completely oblivious to her plight. At this point, she was fully on the edge of her seat, legs fidgeting, sitting upright, and staring intently at the TV. Clearly not relaxed at all. I think she wanted to pass it off as her being really engaged with the show, but I knew better. She was sitting there, gradually getting more and more uncomfortable and not doing a great job hiding it, even though I could tell she was trying.

I thought I was coming to the end of this experience around 3:15, when Ellie stood up. At this point, she had not peed for nearly 6 hours, ever since she left home at 9:30. I thought she finally couldn’t hold it anymore and was getting up to use the bathroom. But nope. She just…..stood there. I surmise that her intent was to go to the bathroom, but she was still too shy and decided against it. I waited and waited for her to make a move towards the bathroom, but she just kept staring directly at the TV. At one point, she bent over to place her hands on her knees. Again, still acting as if she was just really engaged with the show.

The episode then ended, and I thought this would be the point where she would say “OK, I’m gonna go to the bathroom!” But nope. She did a cute little hip dance and said “Next one! Next one!” I was not fooled by this. That was definitely a pee dance. Before I clicked Next Episode, I noticed my computer’s power light was blinking, indicating the need for a charge. I got up and grabbed the charge cable on the other side of the room, deliberately leaving a perfect opening for her to pee. But nope! She just sat there.

At this point, it became clear to me that she was not going to go to the bathroom unless I moved from the bed. It was around 3:20 and she would be leaving after the next episode. As much as the thought of her driving home with an incredibly full bladder turned me on, I didn’t want her to have to go through that, so I decided to be merciful. I casually sat back down on the couch (even though I was way more comfortable lying on the bed), upon which she immediately got up. “Oh wait,” she said. “I’m gonna use the bathroom first.” I fucking knew she had to pee. Fucking knew it.

That bathroom really does have paper thin walls. I could hear her piss jetting out of her like a fire hose from the other side of the room. That, and an audible sigh of relief. When she returned to the couch, her body language was noticeably much more relaxed, and we spent the rest of the time laughing and joking. Afterwards, I hugged her goodbye and wished her a nice date with her new boyfriend the next day.

Ellie has no idea I’m into this kind of stuff, so I doubt she had the foggiest idea how turned on I was by her conundrum, nor that I have the sense to notice the subtle cues of someone needing to pee. Even if not for this, it’s always lovely to see her. She’s a great friend and someone I love having in my life. And I’m so happy she’s finally found a really nice boyfriend.
